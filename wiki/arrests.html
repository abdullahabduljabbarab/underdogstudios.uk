Arrests and the drop-in

Arrests and drop-in built; strike cutscenes in progress

What it does for play. A van that stops can be arrested. That gives the police a second way to score besides wrecking it, and it makes every loading bay a risk. After any strike, a cutscene shows what happened, and the van drops back into the city somewhere new.

How an arrest works

StepWhat happens
ExposedWhenever the van is nearly stopped, or loading at a bay, an amber ring shows on the road round it. The van's screen reads EXPOSED: YOU CAN BE ARRESTED.
ArrestingA cop who stops inside the ring with a clear view of the van starts the arrest. The ring turns red and fills like a clock.
TeamworkA second cop in the ring makes it faster.
EscapingSpeeding up drains the bar. Between the two speed limits it only pauses, so a van being rammed does not flicker in and out.
No cheatingNo arrests through walls, and never on a hidden or cloaked van.
ArrestedThe parcel is seized, each cop in on it is paid, and the van takes a strike.

Loading at a bay takes longer than an arrest, so at a bay the police have to box the van in, not just pull up beside it. Abdullah kept the arrest at 3 seconds for exactly that reason.

The drop-in

StepWhat happens
ChooseThe courier sees the city from above with three numbered drop zones, each away from the cops, the exits and the new pickup, with its distance to the pickup.
PickPress 1, 2 or 3. After 6 seconds the safest is picked.
WarningThe police see VAN LANDING and a ping on their radar 2 seconds before it lands.
LandThe van lands fresh, at full health, with a new job.

Behind the design

Why arrests

Built by Abdullah · Numbers by Dafydd · In-game HUD by Ieuan · Tested by The team (playtests)

Inspired by

Need for Speed: Most Wanted (EA Black Box, 2005): when the player slows down surrounded by police, a bar fills towards BUSTED; driving away empties it. Being caught comes from stopping, not from a health bar.

What we took: the busted meter that fills while stopped. What we changed: the police are real players who have to box the van in together, and a clear view is needed, so walls and smoke matter.

Fortnite (Epic Games, 2017): players choose where to land from the air. The drop-in borrows that moment of choice, but with only three safe options and a time limit, so it stays quick.

Why we chose this

OptionForAgainstVerdict
Wreck onlySimple; already builtPolice gain nothing from a stopped van; bays are safeRejected
Arrest at bays onlyEasy to readA van could stop anywhere else safelyRejected
A ring wherever the van is nearly stoppedEvery stop is a risk; rewards police teamworkNeeds clear on-screen warnings for both sidesChosen

The arrest ring answers the playtest note that the police had no reason to care when the van stopped. Abdullah set the ring at 10 m from the van's centre and asked that everything else stay as tested.

What can go wrong

SituationWhat the game does
A cop rams the van while arrestingThe bar pauses between 8 and 15 km/h instead of resetting
A cop parks behind a wallNo clear view, so no arrest; the cop's screen says NO CLEAR VIEW
The van is cloakedIt cannot be tagged or arrested while hidden
A drop zone is near a copZones are picked away from cops, exits and the pickup, loosening the limits only if fewer than three fit
