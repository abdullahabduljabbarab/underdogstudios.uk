Under the hood

Playtest Record

This is the one running record of every playtest on Shady Shipping, from the prototype sessions in October to the latest uni test. Each row says who played, what they found and what we changed because of it. Together the rows show the game being shaped by play, not only by plan: almost every system in the game changed at least once because someone drove it and told us what was wrong.

Abdullah ran the playtests and wrote up the feedback, and he drove nearly every build himself before it was checked in. Where a list was typed up by someone else, the finding is still credited to the person who played. This follows the playcentric approach in Fullerton (2019): build, play, listen and change, in short loops.

How to read the table. "Uni" means the university labs, with the dedicated server (a PC that only runs the game for everyone else) on Abdullah's home PC unless the row says otherwise. "Sandbox" is the test copy of the project. Several findings from one session have one row each, so every cell stays short. New playtests add rows at the bottom.
