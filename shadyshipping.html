For the courier

How a round plays

  1. 1

    Grab a job

    Pull into a glowing bay and load a parcel. Legal, illegal or hot: you choose the risk.

  2. 2

    Deliver for stars

    Every drop pays. Enough deliveries earn you a star.

  3. 3

    Exits open

    Your first star opens two exits at random. Both sides see them on the radar.

  4. 4

    Smash out

    Hit an open exit's barrier at speed to win. More stars, bigger bonus.

The police: arrest the van or wreck it and that's a strike. Three strikes and the police win.

The big idea

Your cargo is your wanted level

One player drives a dodgy courier van and delivers parcels across the city. Everyone else is the police. What you carry decides what the cops are told about where you are.

LegalSafe · lower pay

The police get nothing. They have to find you with their own eyes.

Cops see: nothing
IllegalRisky · better pay

Every few seconds the police radar shows a circle you're somewhere inside, and it shrinks the longer you carry it.

Cops see: a search circle
HotDouble pay

Jackpot money, but the cops get a blip that follows you round the city.

Cops see: a live blip

Features

What makes it shady

01

Stop, and you're busted

Slow down and an arrest ring opens round the van. A cop who stops inside it with a clear view starts the arrest. Two cops make it faster.

  • Three strikes and the police win
  • An arrest seizes your parcel
  • Speed up to break free
02

Crash it, dent it, wreck it

Every car is a real physics car, and the bodywork crumples where you hit it. Dents land exactly where the impact was, so no two wrecks look the same.

  • Real-time dents on the body and bumpers
  • Bumpers hang off, then fall off
  • Smoke turns black, then the engine catches fire
  • Every screen sees the same damage online

Same fire as the Kingpin’s van. The devs insist this is a coincidence.

03

The drop-in

Get caught and a Shady Shipping cargo flyer lifts your van out. Pick one of three drop zones from the sky and land fresh with a new job.

  • Zones away from the cops and exits
  • The police get a warning just before you land

So THAT’S how he got out.

…hang on. Did the devs just put the escape plan on their own website?

04

The cops play as a team

A lone cop can't win alone. Block the roads, read the radar, share sightings and close the net together.

  • A radar that only shows what your cargo gives away
  • Last-seen markers shared by every cop
05

Make it yours

Two team garages: the courier's chop shop and the police fleet bay. Paint every part, fit body kits and pick a livery.

  • Over a hundred items
  • Buy once, keep for good
  • Looks only. Money never buys a win.

Unlike the Kingpin’s lawyers.
